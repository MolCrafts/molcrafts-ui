VegaView and VegaLiteView render `label` as a visible figure title and `description` as a caption shown once the chart is ready. Add `labelHidden` to keep the label as the accessible name without drawing it. An explicit `width` now includes the frame padding instead of clipping the plot. The default error alert shows the caught message. Axis titles use regular weight.
